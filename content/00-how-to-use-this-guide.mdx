### Reading paths

The modules build on each other, but they are written to survive being read out of order.

- **The full path (00–12).** Start here if you want the mental model rebuilt from the foundation. Part I is conceptual and historical; Parts II and III are operational.
- **The decision path (01, 02, 04, 12).** You have a choice to make this quarter and need the axis, the options and the trade-offs. About twenty minutes.
- **The micro-frontend path (06, 07, 08, 10, 11).** The decision to split the frontend is already made and you need composition, bundling, auth and delivery. This is the densest material here.

### The evidence rule

This is a fast-moving area where a great deal of confidently-written advice is two to five years stale, and where popular tools have been quietly archived or deprecated. So the rule is simple: **any claim a reasonable engineer might dispute carries a link to a primary source** — a spec, an RFC, official documentation, or the maintainers' own words. Secondary sources are labelled as such.

Where a fact could not be confirmed to that standard it is marked inline and collected in <Mod to="caveats" />. That appendix also records claims earlier drafts of this material got *wrong*, because knowing which way the ground moved is often more useful than the corrected fact.

Boxed text marked **From the source** is verbatim from a spec, RFC or official documentation, with a link. Everything else — boxed or not — is my analysis.
