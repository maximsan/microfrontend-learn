<Lede>Almost everything written about “SPA vs. MPA” gets the distinction wrong. It is not about page count. It is about *how long your JavaScript runtime stays alive* — and once you see that, the whole landscape from static HTML to local-first collaboration arranges itself on a single axis, and the architectural decisions stop being fashion.</Lede>

<Lede small>This guide walks that axis end to end: the mechanism, the history that produced it, then the machinery you actually touch — history APIs, micro-frontend composition, bundlers, transports, auth, and delivery. Every non-obvious claim links to a primary source you can check, and every chapter ends at the same question: *where does the long-lived state live, and who is allowed to touch it?*</Lede>
